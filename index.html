Common questions

About VeloraPDF

Are my documents really private?

Yes. The tools are plain JavaScript running on your device; there is no server to receive a file.

Is there a paid plan?

No. Every tool on the site is free and has no watermark or page cap.

Which browsers work?

Any current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari on desktop and mobile.

Can I use it for confidential work?

That is the point of the local-only design — nothing is transmitted, logged, or stored.
